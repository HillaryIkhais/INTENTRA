# INTENTRA — Voiceover Script (with timings)

**Film:** INTENTRA Product Launch (NEAR Hackathon Hero Demo)
**Duration:** 106 seconds (1:46)
**Format:** Spoken narration, conversational and calm. Read with deliberate pauses
around the two rejection moments. No synthetic/robotic voice — record a natural
human take. These timings are the sync reference for the burned-in captions.

---

## SCENE 01 — THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:09)

> You approved one agent.
> You didn't approve an unlimited chain of agents.

---

## SCENE 02 — THE PROBLEM (0:09 – 0:23)

> You give an autonomous agent authority over one NEAR.
> It delegates to another agent. That agent tries to delegate again.
>
> The dangerous part isn't what an agent spends —
> it's what it gives somebody else permission to spend.

---

## SCENE 03 — INTRODUCING INTENTRA (0:23 – 0:34)

> That's the problem INTENTRA is built to solve.
> A financial authority layer for autonomous agents on NEAR.
> Every delegation is constrained by the authority that came before it.

---

## SCENE 04 — THE CEILING RULE (0:34 – 0:48)

> Agent B holds 0.45 NEAR. It tries to create authority worth 0.80 for C.
>
> Rejected. A child can never hold more than its parent.
> Enforced on-chain — proven by twenty-seven adversarial tests.

---

## SCENE 05 — THE DOUBLE-LEDGER (0:48 – 1:02)

> Now Coordinator A tries to spend 0.30 NEAR.
> Its limit is one — ordinary systems allow it.
> But 0.75 is already promised to its children.
> 1.05 against a limit of one. Rejected.

---

## SCENE 06 — THE VALID PATH (1:02 – 1:14)

> Delegation isn't supposed to stop agents from working.
> Agent B pays 0.20. Agent C pays 0.15.
> Both settle — exactly 0.35 NEAR, inside the original boundary.

---

## SCENE 07 — OWNER CONTROL (1:14 – 1:28)

> And what happens when the owner changes their mind?
> The owner revokes the original authority.
> Agent B can no longer execute. Neither can C.
> One action — the chain stops at its source.

---

## SCENE 08 — THE PROOF (1:28 – 1:39)

> This isn't a mock demonstration.
> Deployed on NEAR testnet — all checks passing.
> Eleven evidence checks. Nineteen RPC assertions.
> Twenty-seven tests. Nine recorded transactions.

---

## SCENE 09 — THE CLOSE (1:39 – 1:46)

> Agents can delegate the work.
> They cannot create authority they were never given.
>
> INTENTRA.
> One original budget. Authority that cannot grow.

---

## Recording notes

- Tone: calm confidence, slightly deliberate. Strong emphasis on the opening
  sentence. No exaggerated trailer voice, no corporate urgency.
- Sound design (added in post, not spoken): a precise short sound on each
  rejection; a softer confirmation on each settled payment; a restrained low
  pulse during revocation. Never let sound effects overpower the words.
- Captions are burned into the video (see src/data/captions.ts) so the story
  reads with the sound off.
