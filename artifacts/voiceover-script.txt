# INTENTRA — Narration Script (Product Demonstration)

Read this as a confident, intelligent product demonstration — not a documentary, not a commercial.
Conversational, energetic, natural. Varied pacing. Deliberate pauses on rejections and settlement.

---

[0:00–0:12] — ACT 1: THE PROBLEM AND ORIGINAL AUTHORITY

"Give an agent authority. Let it pass some of that authority to another agent. Then let that one pass it on.

At what point does permission become a blank cheque?

INTENTRA puts a limit on that chain."

---

[0:12–0:28] — ACT 1 CONTINUED: ROOT GRANT

"The owner starts with one NEAR.

Agent A receives it — one NEAR of authority, capped at the original grant.

Every allocation that follows must fit inside this boundary."

---

[0:28–0:42] — ACT 2: FIRST ATTACK — ATTENUATION

"Agent A delegates 0.4 NEAR to B.

Now B tries something different: 0.8 for C.

No. B only inherited 0.4. The contract rejects the expansion.

ATTENUATION."

---

[0:42–0:55] — ACT 3: AGGREGATE BUDGET — BUDGET_RESERVATION

"Then A tries to allocate another 0.7 NEAR.

But 0.4 is already reserved for B.

That would make 1.1 against an original grant of one.

Rejected. BUDGET_RESERVATION."

---

[0:55–1:12] — ACT 3 CONTINUED: THE ARITHMETIC

"Zero point four plus zero point seven equals one point one zero.

The original limit is one point zero zero.

The aggregate stays inside the boundary — always."

---

[1:12–1:30] — ACT 4: VALID DELEGATION AND SETTLEMENT

"Now watch the valid path.

B delegates 0.15 NEAR to C. C stays within that boundary and successfully transfers 0.1 NEAR.

The transfer settles.

Here's the testnet evidence."

---

[1:30–1:43] — ACT 4 CONTINUED: EVIDENCE

"This is not simulated. Recorded on NEAR testnet, verifiable on-chain."

---

[1:43–2:00] — ACT 5: ROOT REVOCATION

"But the owner still has control.

The original grant is revoked.

B tries to act. REVOKED.

C tries too. REVOKED.

The descendants cannot keep using authority that no longer exists."

---

[2:00–2:08] — ACT 5 CONTINUED: TRANSITIVE REVOCATION

"One action at the root. The entire chain is invalidated."

---

[2:08–2:20] — ACT 6: PROOF AND CLOSE

"Deployed on NEAR testnet. Twenty-four contract tests. Twenty-three JSON-RPC checks. Nine live SDK checks.

Agents can delegate authority.

They cannot enlarge it.

INTENTRA."
