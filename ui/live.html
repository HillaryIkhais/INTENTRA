Step 01 Issue Permission
You (the human) give your agent authority to trade.
What's happening: You're telling the engine: "My agent can trade BNBUSDT, only BUY, max $10 per order, max $10 total."
Why it matters: This is the root of the authority chain. Every delegation after this must be a subset of these limits.
Step 02 Delegate
Your agent creates a child agent with narrower authority.
What's happening: The agent wants to delegate execution to a child. INTENTRA checks: is child's authority a subset of parent's?
The invariant: Child ≤ Parent. If the child tries to ask for more than the parent has → BLOCKED.
Step 03 Attack
Try to break the invariant. Give the child more authority than the parent.
What's happening: Select an attack. The engine will check if the child's proposed authority is still a subset of the parent's.
Expected result: All attacks should be BLOCKED. That's the whole point.
Step 04 Validate Trade
Propose a trade. INTENTRA walks the full chain and decides.
What's happening: You propose a trade. INTENTRA checks: is this trade within the authority of the entire chain (human → agent → child)?
Try this: Set amount to $5 (within limits) → ALLOW. Then try $15 (exceeds limit) → BLOCK.
